What’s usually driving it

PCOS is never caused by one thing. That is exactly why a plan that transformed someone else may do nothing at all for you. Working out which driver is dominant in your case is the whole job — and it is the step almost every generic PCOS diet chart skips. Get the driver right and the food work becomes far smaller than you expect.

FAQ

Questions people ask before booking

What is the best diet for PCOS?

There is no single best PCOS diet, and any plan sold as one is guessing. Around seventy per cent of women with PCOS have insulin resistance as the leading driver, but for others inflammation, chronic stress or an untreated thyroid issue is doing more of the work. The right plan follows from identifying which one is dominant in your case, which is why reports come before food changes.

Can PCOS be reversed with diet alone?

PCOS is managed rather than cured, but the symptoms that bother people most — irregular cycles, weight that will not shift, acne, hair fall — often improve substantially when the underlying driver is addressed. Many women see cycle regularity return. Nutrition is one part of that alongside your gynaecologist's care.

Why can I not lose weight with PCOS even when I eat very little?

Eating very little is frequently part of the problem. Chronic under-eating combined with heavy cardio raises stress hormones and lowers thyroid output, which makes weight loss harder in a condition already marked by insulin resistance. The usual correction is eating more protein, more consistently, with strength training replacing some of the cardio.

Do I need to go gluten free or dairy free for PCOS?

Usually not. Blanket gluten and dairy elimination is one of the most repeated pieces of PCOS advice online and one of the least supported for most people. Elimination is used here only where there is a specific reason to suspect it, and always with a plan to reintroduce.

Can I consult you online, or do I need to come to the clinic in Mumbai?

Both are available. Himanshi consults in person in Mumbai and online across India. PCOS work in particular translates well to online consultations, since it depends on reports, cycle tracking and regular check-ins rather than physical examination.

Will this help if I am trying to conceive?

Nutrition support for PCOS commonly runs alongside fertility treatment, and improving insulin sensitivity and cycle regularity is often part of that picture. Any fertility decisions stay entirely with your gynaecologist or fertility specialist.
