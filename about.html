Her Story

The diagnosis that changed everything

At sixteen, Himanshi was diagnosed with PCOS: a delayed cycle, weight gain, and a body that felt entirely out of her control.

What actually changed things wasn't a strict diet or a quick fix. It was motivation: to eat cleaner, move her body, and finally take her stress seriously. Because PCOS is never caused by one simple thing. Not insulin resistance alone. Not weight. Not "bad" eating habits. It's rooted in the underlying cause, and that's exactly what she'd been missing all along.

That realisation is the foundation of how we work with every client today: look past the obvious symptom, find what's actually driving it, and build habits around that truth.

Why work this way

Nutrition advice is loud. This is the quiet, useful version.

Five things we hold onto in every plan we build.

01

Because your body isn't a checklist

Blood sugar, gut symptoms, hormones, and eating patterns are treated as connected, not as four separate problems handed to four separate specialists.

02

Because restriction isn't the goal

No elimination diets by default, no fear-based food rules. Just what the evidence actually supports for your labs and your life.

03

Because your relationship with food matters too

How you think about food shapes whether a plan sticks. That psychology is part of the work, not an afterthought.

04

Because understanding beats obedience

You'll know why a recommendation makes sense for your body, not just what to do, so you can keep making good calls on your own.

05

Because consistency beats perfection

Small, sustainable shifts that survive a bad week, a holiday, or a stretch of takeout. Not a plan that only works in ideal conditions.

Every symptom is information, not a failure. When the body is out of balance, it says so, and our job is just to listen closely enough to hear it.
On the philosophy behind this work

This is what we have seen again and again: lasting health isn't found in a single fix. It's restoring balance in the body, and in the way we live day to day.
