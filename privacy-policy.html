Privacy Policy

What happens to the information you share with us, in plain language.

Last updated: 9 September 2026

Section 01Who we are

Health with Himanshi ("we," "us," "our") is a nutrition and dietetics practice run by Himanshi Daryani. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have. You can reach us anytime at healthwithhimanshi@gmail.com.

Section 02Information we collect

We collect information in the following ways:

  • Contact form submissions: name, email address, and any message or health-related details you choose to share when enquiring.
  • Consultation & intake information: if you book a session, we may collect health history, lab reports, dietary habits, and related personal health information necessary to provide nutrition counselling.
  • Communication records: messages exchanged via email, WhatsApp, or messaging tools used to support your program.
  • Website usage data: basic technical data (browser type, pages visited) if analytics tools are added in future.

Section 03How we use your information

  • To respond to enquiries and schedule consultations
  • To provide personalized nutrition counselling and track your progress
  • To communicate with you about your program
  • To improve our services and website

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

Section 04Health information

Because our services involve nutrition counselling, we may collect sensitive health-related information (e.g. diagnoses like PCOS or diabetes, lab values, medication). This information is used solely to provide you with personalized guidance and is kept confidential. It is not shared with any third party without your explicit consent, except where required by law.

Section 05Data sharing

We may share information with:

  • Service providers who help us operate (e.g. scheduling or payment tools), bound by confidentiality obligations
  • Legal or regulatory authorities where required by law

We do not share your health information with any third party for marketing purposes.

Section 06Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your information
  • Withdraw consent for future communication

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at healthwithhimanshi@gmail.com.

Section 07Cookies & analytics

Our website may use cookies and third-party analytics tools (e.g., Google Analytics) to understand visitor behavior and improve the site. You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings.

Section 08Data security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Section 09Children’s privacy

Our services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under 18 without parental consent.

Section 10Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated “Last updated” date.

Section 11Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at healthwithhimanshi@gmail.com.
