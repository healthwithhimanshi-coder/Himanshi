Privacy Policy

What happens to the information you share with us, in plain language.

Last updated: 31 August 2026

A note from the developer: this is a working template written to match how the site actually behaves. Please read it through, correct anything that doesn't reflect your practice, and have it reviewed by a professional before relying on it — especially if you take clients outside India.

Who we are

Health with Himanshi (“we”, “us”) is a nutrition counselling practice run by Himanshi Daryani. This policy explains what happens to information you share through this website. You can reach us at healthwithhimanshi@gmail.com with any question about it.

What we collect

Through the enquiry form on this site, we collect only what you type into it:

  • Your first and last name
  • Your email address
  • Your mobile number, if you choose to provide it
  • The topic you select, and anything you write in the message field

We do not run analytics, advertising trackers, or third-party cookies on this website. The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means Google receives your IP address as part of serving those files.

How your information is used

Enquiry details are used for one purpose: to reply to you and, if you go on to work with us, to arrange that. We do not sell your information, and we do not add you to marketing lists without asking you first.

How it reaches us

The enquiry form is delivered by FormSubmit, a third-party form service, which passes your message to our email inbox. Your message therefore passes through their systems and ours in transit.

Health information

Please do not send detailed medical history, lab reports or clinical documents through the website form. If you become a client, we will tell you how to share those securely.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not become client relationships are kept only as long as needed to respond, and are deleted periodically. Client records are retained as required for professional and legal purposes.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask us to delete it. Write to healthwithhimanshi@gmail.com and we will respond within a reasonable period.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will appear on this page with an updated date at the top.
