Questions

The things people ask first

Fees, how consultations run, what changes and what doesn't. If something isn't covered here, just ask.

Before you book

How much does it cost?

The Reset Program is priced as a complete programme rather than by the session, because that's how the work actually happens: sessions, plan, messaging support and check-ins together. Exact fees depend on which programme fits, and are shared on the discovery call so you know before committing to anything.

What happens in the first conversation?

It's a conversation, not a sales call. Himanshi asks what's going on, what you've already tried, and what your reports say. By the end you'll have an honest answer about whether she is the right person to help — including if the answer is no.

Do you consult online or in person?

Both. Online consultations work over video and cover clients anywhere, and in-person sessions are available too. The programme is identical either way; the only difference is where you sit for it.

Do I need blood tests before starting?

Not to start. If you already have recent reports, bring them — they make the first conversation far more useful. If you don't, Himanshi will tell you which ones are worth asking your doctor for, and why each one matters.

How the work actually goes

Will I get a diet chart?

Not the printed one-size chart you may have been handed before. You get a plan built around your labs, your routine, your kitchen and what you'll realistically eat — which is the only kind anyone follows past the second week.

Do I have to give up rice, roti or my favourite foods?

No. Blanket elimination is not the default here. How much, when, and what a food is eaten alongside usually matters more than removing it. Where something genuinely does need restricting — a low-FODMAP phase, for instance — it's temporary and diagnostic, with a plan to widen the diet again.

How long before I see a difference?

That depends on what's being worked on, your medical history, and how consistent things are. Anyone promising you a fixed number in a fixed timeframe is guessing. What you'll get here is a clear view of which markers should move first and roughly what a realistic pace looks like for your situation.

I travel constantly and eat out a lot. Does that rule me out?

The opposite — it's specifically built into the programme. Travel guidance, eating-out strategies and grocery swaps are part of the work, because a plan that only survives a quiet week at home isn't a plan.

Can you work with vegetarian, Jain or eggless diets?

Yes. Plans are built around how you already eat, not around a template that assumes otherwise. Protein targets in particular need real thought on a vegetarian or Jain diet, and that's part of the job.

Medical questions

Will you change my medication?

No, never. Medication decisions belong to your doctor, and Himanshi will not tell you to stop, start, or adjust a dose. Nutrition care runs alongside your medical treatment, and where something needs a doctor's attention you'll be told to go back to them.

Do you work with my doctor?

Wherever possible, yes. Many clients arrive by referral from a gynaecologist, endocrinologist or gastroenterologist, and the work is far better when both sides can see the same picture.

Can you help with type 1 diabetes?

Yes — this is a specialist area, not an afterthought. Himanshi is a certified Type 1 Diabetes Educator (SPEED). The work covers carb counting you can trust, how fat and protein delay a rise, planning around exercise, and eating out without losing control. It always works alongside your endocrinologist and your prescribed insulin, never in place of either.

Is this only for weight loss?

No, and weight is often the least interesting part. Most clients come for blood sugar, gut symptoms, PCOS or cholesterol. Where weight is the goal, the first step is usually ruling out what's driving it — thyroid, PCOS, insulin resistance — before anyone talks about eating less.

Something not answered here? Ask Himanshi directly — questions before booking are welcome, and always answered by her.
