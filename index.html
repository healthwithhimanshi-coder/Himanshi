Her Story

This started with her own diagnosis

Himanshi was sixteen when she was diagnosed with PCOS. A delayed cycle, weight gain, and a body that felt entirely out of her control.

What eventually changed things wasn't a strict diet or a quick fix. It was understanding that PCOS is never caused by one simple thing, and that the answer sat underneath the symptoms rather than on top of them. That realisation became the way she works with every client now.
