My Story

The diagnosis that changed everything

At sixteen, I was diagnosed with PCOS — a delayed cycle, weight gain, and a body that felt entirely out of my control.

What actually changed things wasn't a strict diet or a quick fix. It was motivation — to eat cleaner, move my body, and finally take my stress seriously. Because PCOS is never caused by one simple thing. Not insulin resistance alone. Not weight. Not "bad" eating habits. It's rooted in the underlying cause — and that's what I'd been missing all along.

That realization is the foundation of how I work with every client today: look past the obvious symptom, find what's actually driving it, and build habits around that truth.

Why work this way

Nutrition advice is loud. This is the quiet, useful version.

Five things I try to hold onto in every plan I build.

01

Because your body isn't a checklist

Blood sugar, gut symptoms, hormones, and eating patterns are treated as connected — not four separate problems handed to four separate specialists.

02

Because restriction isn't the goal

No elimination diets by default, no fear-based food rules. Just what the evidence actually supports for your labs and your life.

03

Because your relationship with food matters too

How you think about food shapes whether a plan sticks. That psychology is part of the work, not an afterthought.

04

Because understanding beats obedience

You'll know why a recommendation makes sense for your body, not just what to do — so you can keep making good calls without me.

05

Because consistency beats perfection

Small, sustainable shifts that survive a bad week, a holiday, or a stretch of takeout — not a plan that only works in ideal conditions.
